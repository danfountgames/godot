# GOALS

Player-facing outcomes derived from `docs/GAME_SPEC.md`. Outcomes, not coding tasks.

Statuses: `UNSCOPED`, `READY`, `IN_PROGRESS`, `OBSERVED`, `VERIFIED`, `BLOCKED`.
`OBSERVED` means it worked once. `VERIFIED` means it works, is tested, its failure and
edge cases pass, its evidence is inspected and indexed, and no critical or major issue
remains. `BLOCKED` is only for a genuinely external condition.
